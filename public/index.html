MasterJi Privacy Policy & User Data Guidelines

Complete transparency regarding how MasterJi collects, processes, protects, and deletes user data.

App: MasterJi (OMR Sheet Scanner) Target: Educators & Teachers (18+) Effective Date: November 2026 Version: 1.0.0

🛡️ 1. Introduction & Overview

MasterJi ("we", "our", or "us") is an assessment utility designed to help teachers, schools, and coaching educators grade physical OMR sheets quickly and accurately using computer vision. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting all personal information.

Core Privacy Principle: MasterJi does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or student data. All optical mark recognition (OMR) calculations and bubble image detections occur 100% on your device using client-side OpenCV algorithms.

📋 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information strictly necessary to provide authentication, device licensing, and app functionality:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Full Name: Collected during registration to display on your educator profile and printed report headers.
  • Mobile Phone Number: Acts as your unique account credential for authentication and account recovery.
  • Password Hash: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 10) before reaching storage. We never store or transmit your raw password.
  • School Name (Optional): Stored locally or optionally provided in profile settings for evaluation reports.

B. Device & Licensing Identifiers

  • Device Identifier: A unique UUID generated once upon installation to bind license keys to your phone, preventing unauthorized account sharing or license tampering.
  • Authentication Tokens & Secret Keys: Standard JSON Web Tokens (5-day expiration) and opaque license tokens (e.g. sk_demo_... or sk_paid_...) stored securely inside the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.

C. Scoring Data & Answer Keys

  • Exam question configurations, answer keys, subject names, and student scores that you record are stored locally on your device (in masterji/exams.json).

📷 3. Camera & Photo Library Access

To detect and score bubbles on physical OMR answer sheets, MasterJi requests permission to use your Camera and Photo Library:

Zero Cloud Image Upload:

The OMR image detection pipeline (Hough circle detection, perspective correction, grid grouping, and Otsu dark-ratio scoring) runs entirely locally on your smartphone processor via OpenCV Dart. We do NOT upload raw student exam sheet photos to any remote server or external cloud AI service.

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to take live photos of student answer sheets.
  • Photo Library Access: Required only when you choose to import an existing answer sheet photo from your gallery.
  • Image Retention: Processed sheets are held in temporary memory during scoring and are saved in local application storage only if you choose to record the session.

🔒 4. Data Storage and Protection

We implement comprehensive security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encrypted Transport: All API communication between the mobile app and server is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS.
  • Hardware-Backed Keystore: Sensitive credentials (JWT tokens, secret keys) are stored on the device using FlutterSecureStorage backed by the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
  • Minimal Server Footprint: Scans, image buffers, and scoring calculations do not require continuous server round-trips.

🗑️ 5. Account & Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy on account deletion, MasterJi provides permanent account and data deletion both inside the app and via this public webpage.

Permanent Deletion Warning

Deleting your MasterJi account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your user profile data, active licence keys, scan credits, and server records will be permanently erased from our database.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account directly inside the MasterJi app in seconds:

  1. Open the MasterJi app on your mobile device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down past the "Log out" button and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Read the confirmation prompt and tap "Delete Forever".
  5. Your account and database records will be erased immediately, and all local credentials will be wiped from your device.

Method 2: Web-Based Account Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your phone, you can request manual account deletion using the form below or by emailing our support team:

Request Account Deletion via Email

Send an email to our support team with your registered mobile number, and we will permanently delete your account within 48 hours.

MasterJi does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Any student scores evaluated using the scanner remain stored locally under the control of the educator.
