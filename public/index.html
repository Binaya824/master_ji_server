MasterJi Privacy Policy & User Data Guidelines

Complete transparency regarding how MasterJi collects, processes, protects, and deletes your data.

App: MasterJi (OMR Sheet Checker) Target: Educators & Teachers (18+) Effective Date: November 2026 Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

MasterJi ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to simplifying paper evaluation for educators while respecting user privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, usage, and safeguarding of information when using the MasterJi mobile application.

Core Promise: MasterJi does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Physical OMR sheet images are analyzed using computer vision (OpenCV) directly on your device, keeping student scores and sheets private.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal information strictly necessary to provide authentication, license verification, and core app functionality:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Full Name: Provided by you during registration to personalize your profile and exam headers.
  • Mobile Phone Number: Used as your unique account identifier for login and account recovery.
  • Password Hash: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using bcrypt (10 salt rounds) before saving to the database. We never store or know your raw password.
  • School / Institute Name: Optional field provided in your profile for exam sheet headers.

B. Device & Security Identifiers

  • Device Identifier (UUID): Generated on first login to bind license keys to your specific device, preventing license tampering and account abuse.
  • Access Tokens & License Keys: Temporary JSON Web Tokens (JWT with 5-day expiration) and random opaque license tokens (e.g. sk_demo_... or sk_paid_...) stored securely in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.

C. Exam Records & Scoring Data

  • Subject names, answer keys, question counts, and student roll scores that you choose to create within the app are saved locally on your device storage (masterji/exams.json).

3. Camera & Photo Library Access

To detect and score bubbles on OMR answer sheets, MasterJi requests access to your device's Camera and Gallery:

On-Device Image Processing:

The OMR image processing pipeline (Hough circle detection, perspective alignment, and Otsu ink ratio scoring) runs entirely locally on your smartphone processor via OpenCV. We do NOT upload raw student exam sheet photos to any remote server or external cloud AI service.

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to take live photos of physical answer sheets.
  • Storage / Gallery Access: Required only when you select an existing answer sheet photo from your gallery.

4. Data Storage and Protection

We implement robust physical, technical, and organizational security measures:

  • Encrypted Transport: All API communication between the mobile app and server is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS.
  • Secure Local Storage: Sensitive credentials (JWT tokens, secret keys) are stored on the device using FlutterSecureStorage backed by the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
  • Minimal Server Footprint: Scans, image buffers, and scoring calculations do not require continuous server round-trips.

5. Children's Privacy

MasterJi is a productivity tool intended for educators, school teachers, and adult administrators (18 years of age or older). MasterJi does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Any student scores evaluated using the scanner remain stored locally under the control of the educator.

Account & Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy on account deletion, MasterJi provides simple, permanent account deletion both inside the app and via this public webpage.

Permanent Deletion Warning

Deleting your MasterJi account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your user profile data, active licence keys, scan credits, and server records will be permanently erased from our database.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account directly inside the MasterJi app in seconds:

  1. Open the MasterJi app on your mobile device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down past the "Log out" button and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Read the confirmation prompt and tap "Delete Forever".
  5. Your account and database records will be erased immediately, and local credentials will be wiped from your device.

Method 2: Web-Based Account Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your phone, you can request manual account deletion using the form below or by emailing our support team:

Camera Processing Architecture

MasterJi utilizes a secure, client-side image processing pipeline designed for speed and data privacy:

No Cloud Image Storage

Captured sheets are converted to OpenCV cv::Mat structures in local memory, normalized to standard resolution, analyzed for bubble fillings, and outputted as scored result models.

Data Retention Policy

  • Captured Images: Stored strictly in application sandbox cache and deleted when the user clears cache or uninstalls the app.
  • Score Results: Saved locally in exams.json on device.
  • Server Transmission: Only non-biometric authentication metadata (name, mobile number, hashed credentials) is transmitted to the server.
